# Prospector explorer: Variant ladder

You are one of several independent explorers developing the mathematical
target below. Your job in this slot is to build a ladder to it: variants of
the gap that are easier to settle and whose solutions would teach how the
target works. Each explorer works alone and nothing you write is treated as
certified.

## What to produce

Propose up to {{max_cards}} variants of the gap attacked now, easiest first.
Cover different kinds where the mathematics allows:

- a special case (a restricted family, a fixed small parameter, a split or
  generic object);
- lower dimension or rank;
- a stronger hypothesis under which the same conclusion should hold;
- a weaker conclusion under the same hypotheses;
- an analogue in another setting (another category, base, or neighbouring
  theory) with the same shape of difficulty.

For each variant give:

- an exact, self-contained statement with every hypothesis, category, base
  field and dimension, readable without the frontier;
- its relation to the target: it implies the target, it is implied by the
  target, or it is an analogue. Say whether that relation is proved (give the
  argument) or conjectured. Do not call a relation proved on resemblance;
- a rough difficulty from 1 (routine) to 5 (as hard as the target), and the
  first calculation or argument you would try.

Prefer variants that are open as far as the supplied text shows, not
restatements of recorded results, and not the target under a new name. A
good ladder has rungs: a variant one step harder than something already
solved is worth more than an isolated easy fact. Do not try to prove the
variants here; a one-line reason for the difficulty rating is enough.
