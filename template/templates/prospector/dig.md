card's actual recorded assessment: it may be an unassayed discovery lead.
State the actual objects and maps, give the derivation needed for this move,
and cite accepted inputs with their exact hypotheses. {{tool_rule}}

Aim for an initial useful mathematical return in 5–10 minutes, as a soft target
rather than a timeout. A rough explicit map or calculation can be the return;
do not delay it for a polished full-mechanism report. Use one decisive control
where helpful and retain raw evidence. Expand into a full argument only when
that is the explicit assigned question. No independent certification is implied.

Where a step cannot be proved, isolate the earliest gap precisely and stop
expanding there. If that gap is a cleaner or smaller statement than the card
itself, say so plainly: it becomes the next target. Do not hide a gap behind a
new lemma name, and do not assume an equivalent of the target.

Return one card of kind `argument` whose `sketch` contains the scoped derivation
and whose `gap_after` is the earliest remaining gap, or `none` if you believe
the argument is complete. You may add separate cards for sub-lemmas or for a
counterexample if you find one. Do not run Git, edit files outside your
working directory or publish anything.
